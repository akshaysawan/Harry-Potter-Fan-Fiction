Group 1: The Newlyweds (The Six)

Members: Harry Potter & Hermione Granger , Draco Malfoy & Ginny Weasley , Ron Weasley & Luna Lovegood.

Role: The protagonists and the heart of the new era. They are the ones navigating careers, marriage, and the post-war world.

Key Dynamics:

Harry & Draco: Went from enemies to brothers-in-arms. Draco bought the engagement ring for Harry; Harry saved Draco’s life/soul.

The "Ostrich" Club: Hermione and Ginny both use the "bury face in partner's chest" maneuver to hide from embarrassment.

The Financials: They all wield the "Black Card" (Draco's infinite Gringotts credit line).

Group 2: The Coalition of Parents (The Management)

Members: Vernon & Petunia Dursley , Lucius & Narcissa Malfoy , Arthur & Molly Weasley , Xenophilius Lovegood.

Role: The logistical engine. They organize the events, handle the finances, and provide the comedy of clashing cultures.

Key Dynamics:

The "Triple Merger": Vernon and Lucius have formed a terrifyingly efficient partnership based on business, tax evasion, and "efficiency".

The Tech Enthusiasts: Arthur and Lucius bond over Muggle technology (spark plugs, staplers, planes).

The Matriarchs: Molly, Narcissa, and Petunia bond over household management and keeping the men in line.

Group 3: The High Table (The Faculty)

Members: Albus Dumbledore , Minerva McGonagall , Severus Snape.

Role: The heavy hitters. They provide magical muscle, wisdom, and sarcasm.

Key Traits:

The Titanium Livers: Dumbledore and McGonagall can out-drink anyone (including the "Voldemort" cocktail) without getting hungover.

The Dungeon Sprinter: Snape is secretly incredibly fit (ran a 4-minute mile on sand).

Dumbledore's Style: Now prefers Hawaiian shirts and sequins over formal robes.

Group 4: The Cavalry (The Survivors)

Members: Sirius Black , Remus Lupin , Nymphadora Tonks , Fred & George Weasley , Rubeus Hagrid , Dobby.

Role: The chaotic good. They bring the fun, the noise, and the emotional support.

Key Dynamics:

The Marauders & Snape: Sirius and Remus have fully reconciled with Snape after he broke down at the wedding; they now function as a dysfunctional brotherhood.

The Consultants: Dobby is a paid "Freelance Consultant" who manages logistics for Draco.

The Twins & Dudley: Fred, George, and Dudley bond over physical comedy and explosions.
